Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Transparent data protection, GDPR compliant.

Last Updated: August 2026

Rapid Car Rentals operates rapidnrental.com and provides car rental services across the United States and Europe. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information.

When you book a vehicle, we collect your full name, date of birth, email address, phone number, home address, driver's license number, International Driving Permit (for Europe rentals), passport or ID number, emergency contact information, rental preferences, and special requests. For payment, we collect credit/debit card details (processed securely via Stripe; we do not store full card numbers), billing address, and payment method preference.

When you visit our website, we automatically collect your IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, pages visited, time spent on each page, referring website, links clicked, search terms used, and location data derived from your IP address (not precise GPS). We may receive information about you from credit bureaus for payment verification, driver's license verification services to confirm age and licensing status, background check providers for fraud prevention, insurance partners (Acko) for coverage verification, payment processors (Stripe) for transaction verification, international banking partners (Wise) for international transfer records, and government agencies for customs and immigration data.

We use your information to process rental bookings, verify your identity and legal eligibility to rent, arrange vehicle pickup and return, process payments via Stripe, and manage your reservations. We respond to your inquiries, resolve complaints and disputes, provide technical support, send confirmations and receipts, and process refunds or cancellations. For safety and fraud prevention, we verify your eligibility, detect fraudulent bookings, prevent identity theft, monitor for unauthorized access, and assess rental risk based on damage history and accident patterns.

We document insurance coverage, process damage claims with photos and repair estimates, manage chargeback disputes if you dispute a charge with your bank, investigate accidents, and calculate liability for damage. We send you booking confirmations (automatic and required), rental reminders with pickup details, rental receipts via email for your accounting, promotional offers only if you opted in, collect feedback through post-rental surveys, and send newsletters if you subscribed. You can opt out of marketing emails anytime by clicking "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any email.

We track website performance to see which pages work well, identify user behavior trends, improve user experience and site speed, conduct A/B testing of different versions, and generate reports on monthly traffic, conversion rates, and revenue. For legal and compliance purposes, we comply with tax regulations, traffic laws, and rental laws, respond to subpoenas if legally required by law enforcement, defend against legal claims if necessary, and maintain audit trails for regulatory inspections.

We never sell or share your personal data with marketing companies, data brokers, third-party advertisers (except for remarketing pixels), competitors, or unaffiliated businesses without your consent. We do share your information with Stripe for PCI-DSS Level 1 certified credit card processing, Acko for insurance coverage and claims, Wise for international fund transfers and refunds, driver's license verification services to confirm you're legally able to rent, address verification services to confirm your identity, law enforcement if legally required by warrant or subpoena, tax authorities for income and sales tax reporting, customs and immigration agencies for international travel documentation, email services to send confirmations and newsletters, Google Analytics to track website traffic and user behavior, and customer support tools like CRM systems and ticketing systems.

You have the right to request and download all personal information we hold about you. To request, email support@rapidnrental.com with subject "Request for My Personal Data" and we will respond within 30 days. If your information is inaccurate, you can request corrections by contacting us at support@rapidnrental.com and we'll update it within 5 business days. You can request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal requirements. We may retain data if required by law (tax records, insurance claims), needed for ongoing disputes, still relevant to your rental account, or required for fraud prevention. To request deletion, email support@rapidnrental.com with subject "Request for Data Deletion" and we'll respond within 30 days.

You can request your data in a standard electronic format (.CSV, .JSON) to transfer to another company by emailing support@rapidnrental.com with subject "Request for Data Portability." To stop receiving marketing emails, click "Unsubscribe" at the bottom of any marketing email or email us at support@rapidnrental.com. Your request takes effect within 48 hours. You'll still receive transactional emails like booking confirmations, receipts, and important notices.

We use HTTPS (SSL/TLS) encryption to encrypt data in transit. Stripe uses PCI-DSS Level 1 encryption for payment data. Sensitive data is encrypted at rest. Only authorized employees access personal data, passwords are required for all systems, we conduct regular security audits, and we perform background checks for staff. Your credit card never touches our servers. Stripe handles all payment processing with real-time fraud detection, 3D Secure additional authentication, chargeback protection, and full compliance with Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).

We retain payment information for 7 years for tax and fraud prevention purposes. Rental bookings are retained for 7 years for dispute resolution and records. Insurance claims are retained for 10 years for legal liability. Website analytics are retained for 26 months (Google Analytics default). Emails are retained for 3 years for customer service and support. Cookies vary in retention. Marketing lists are retained until you unsubscribe.

If your data is compromised, we will investigate immediately within 24 hours, notify you via email within 72 hours (GDPR requirement), explain what data was affected, provide steps you can take to protect yourself, offer free credit monitoring if applicable, and report to regulatory authorities if required.

If you're a European resident, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR): the right of access to get a copy of your data, the right of rectification to correct inaccurate data, the right of erasure or "right to be forgotten" to request deletion, the right to restrict processing to limit how we use your data, the right to data portability to get your data in portable format, the right to object to certain types of processing, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. We process booking information under the legal basis of contract to process your rental. Payment data is processed under contract and legal basis to process payment and comply with tax law. Insurance details are processed under contract and legal basis to manage insurance and handle claims. Marketing emails are processed based on your consent to send promotional offers. Analytics are processed under legitimate interest to improve website experience. Fraud prevention is processed under legitimate interest to protect against theft and misuse.

If you have concerns about how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with your country's data protection authority: Germany (Bundesdatenschutzbeauftragte), France (CNIL), Italy (Autorità Garante), Spain (Agencia Española de Protección de Datos), or UK (Information Commissioner's Office). For data transfers outside the EU, we use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure your data meets EU protection standards.

If you're a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA): the right to know what personal data we collect, the right to delete your data, the right to opt out of sale (we don't sell data, so this doesn't apply), and the right to non-discrimination meaning we won't penalize you for exercising your rights. To submit a CCPA request, email support@rapidnrental.com with subject "CCPA Request - [Right to Know / Right to Delete]" and include your name, email, and phone number. We'll verify your identity and respond within 45 days.

We use session cookies to keep you logged in (expire when browser closes), persistent cookies to remember your choices (1 year duration), Google Analytics to track site usage (2 years), Stripe pixels to track conversions (2 years), and marketing cookies to retarget ads (1-2 years). You can disable cookies in Chrome by going to Settings → Privacy → Cookies → Block all cookies. In Safari go to Preferences → Privacy → Cookies → "Never". In Firefox go to Preferences → Privacy → Uncheck "Accept cookies". In Edge go to Settings → Privacy → Cookies → Block all cookies. Note that disabling cookies may prevent our website from working properly for bookings, login, and payment processing.

Our services are not intended for children under 18, as the legal rental age is 18-21 depending on jurisdiction. If you are under 18, do not provide personal information and do not attempt to book a rental. If we discover a child has provided data, we'll delete it immediately and notify the parent or guardian.

Our website may link to third-party websites like Google Maps and payment providers. This Privacy Policy does not apply to websites you access through external links, third-party services like Stripe, Wise, and Acko, social media platforms, or government websites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices so please review their privacy policies independently.

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages (US-based). If you're in the EU or UK, your data may be transferred to the US. We protect international transfers with Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs), encryption in transit, and strict access controls.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect new laws or regulations, changes to our business practices, technical improvements, or user feedback. We'll notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website, updating the "Last Updated" date, and sending you an email if required by law. Your continued use of our services means acceptance of the updated policy.
